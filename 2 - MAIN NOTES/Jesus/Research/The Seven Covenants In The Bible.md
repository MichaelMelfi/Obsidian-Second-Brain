## 🌍 1. **Adam/Creation Covenant** (Genesis 1–2)

Also called the **Edenic Covenant**

### With: **Adam (and all humanity)**

### Key Points:

- Be fruitful and multiply
    
- Have dominion over the earth
    
- Do not eat from the Tree of Knowledge
    
- Death is the consequence of disobedience
    

> ❗️Broken when Adam and Eve sinned (Genesis 3)

---

## 🔥 2. **Adamic Covenant** (Genesis 3)

This is God’s covenant **after the Fall** — includes judgment and promise.

### With: **Adam and Eve** (and humanity)

### Key Points:

- Curse on the serpent and ground
    
- Pain in childbirth, struggle in work
    
- Promise of a **coming Redeemer** (Genesis 3:15 – the “protoevangelium”)
    

---

## 🌈 3. **Noahic Covenant** (Genesis 9:8–17)

### With: **Noah and all living creatures**

### Key Points:

- Never again will God destroy the earth with a flood
    
- Rainbow as the sign
    
- Affirms value of human life and stewardship
    

---

## ⭐ 4. **Abrahamic Covenant** (Genesis 12, 15, 17)

### With: **Abraham and his descendants**

### Key Points:

- God will make Abraham a great nation
    
- Land (Canaan) as an inheritance
    
- All nations will be blessed through his seed (Messiah)
    
- Circumcision as the sign
    

---

## 📜 5. **Mosaic Covenant** (Exodus 19–24)

Also called the **Sinai Covenant**

### With: **Israel** (through Moses)

### Key Points:

- Ten Commandments + full Law
    
- Blessings for obedience, curses for disobedience
    
- Sacrificial system to atone for sin
    
- Conditional covenant
    

---

## 👑 6. **Davidic Covenant** (2 Samuel 7; Psalm 89)

### With: **King David and his royal line**

### Key Points:

- David’s descendants will rule forever
    
- A future King (Messiah) will come from his line
    
- Eternal throne and kingdom
    

---

## 🩸 7. **New Covenant** (Jeremiah 31:31–34; Luke 22:20; Hebrews 8)

### With: **All who believe in Christ**

### Key Points:

- Law written on hearts, not just stone
    
- Forgiveness of sins
    
- Intimate relationship with God
    
- Jesus’ blood as the sign
    
- Fulfillment of all previous covenants
