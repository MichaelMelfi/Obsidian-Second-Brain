## Types of Psalms
How to read them.
#### Laments
Corporate and individual cry out to God.
Psalm 22
#### Praise
Which call Gods people to admire His great attributes and deeds.
Psalm 145-147
#### Thanksgiving
A Psalm of Thanksgiving has similarities with a psalm of praise but is more personal.
Psalm 33
#### Salvation - History
Review the history of Gods saving works among the people of Israel.
Psalm 22
#### Celebration
Several Kinds
- royal
- Messianic
- Songs of Zion
#### Trust
Center around the fact that God can be trusted in any circumstance
Psalm 11
#### Imprecatory
Expressions of extreme emotions. Which we often find hard to accept in the scriptures
Psalms 137:7-9

## How to use them
#### Laments
Psalm 3
Corporate and individual cry out to God
- Address - To the Lord
- Complaint - Honest identification of trouble
- Trust - Immediately expresses trust in God
- Deliverance - Cry out to God for deliverance from the situation
- Assurance - That God will deliver parallel to trust
- Praise - Thanking and honouring God for the blessings of the past/present/future

#### Thanksgiving
Psalm 138
Has similarities with a psalm of praise, but is more personal.
- Introduction - Testimony of how God has helped
- Distress - The situation from which God have deliverance is portrayed
- Appeal - Reiterated appeal that was made by God
- Deliverance - Which God provided is described
- Testimony - A word of praise for God’s mercy is given
