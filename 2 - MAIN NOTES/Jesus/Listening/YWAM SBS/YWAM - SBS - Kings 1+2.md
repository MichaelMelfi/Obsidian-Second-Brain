### Persia
**Capital:** Persepolis (ceremonial capital), Susa, and Pasargadae
**History:** Rose from a Persian kingdom under Cyrus and conquered Babylon.
**Culture:**
- Highly organized administration
- Extensive road networks and postal system
- Multi-ethnic and multilingual empire
- Strong emphasis on law, order, and governance
**Distinctive Feature:**
- Generally allowed conquered peoples to keep their customs, languages, and religions
- Known for relatively tolerant rule compared to previous empires
**Religion:**
- Associated with Zoroastrianism
- Empire contained many religions and ethnic groups
**Biblical Significance:**
- Allowed the Jewish exiles to return to Jerusalem
- Supported the rebuilding of the Second Temple
- Empire of Ezra, Nehemiah, Esther, Haggai, and Zechariah
#### Key Dates
- 559 BC → Cyrus the Great becomes king of Persia

- 539 BC → Babylon falls to Persia
- 538 BC → Cyrus permits the Jewish exiles to return to Judah
- 516 BC → Second Temple completed in Jerusalem
- 486–465 BC → Reign of Xerxes I (commonly associated with Esther)
- 465–424 BC → Reign of Artaxerxes I (Ezra and Nehemiah period)
- 331 BC → Alexander the Great defeats Persia
- 330 BC → Persian Empire falls to Greece
