## The different views on Chronicles book
### 1 Historical account
Good about the view
- Throughout history most have viewed this book through this lens
- The book seems to be a retelling of stories from Kings

Bad about the view
- If the account necessary?
- If it is just historical account then it is not necessary as we have kings.
- Selectiveness of material brought up
- When compared to Deuteronomy - kings is very distinctive and narrowly focused
- Preoccupation with temple

### 2 Theology Book
Good about the view
- Some scholars see it as a “Through and through a theology essay”
- Seems to treat history as a means to an end rather than an end itself (Looking at the meanings of the history not just the history itself)
- Interest in speeches ad prayers
- Does not reference his own time-emphasis on showing the meaning in Israels past

Bad about the view
- The author never states this as his aim
- Overlooks the attention the author gives to the Davidic monarchy
- Extended parable on seeking God
- Points to restoration through Exile and judgment
- God given way of atonement for Israels guilt
- Points to restoration through exile and judgment
- The scholars fail to agree on emphasis in theology

### 3 Sermon
Good about the view
- Frequency in speeches
- Scholars “his whole work is a Leviticus sermon”
- Warning and encouraging to responsive faith
- Levitical sermon based on the speeches being very preachy or homiletic in style
- Levites teaching role are emphasised

Bad about the view
- Questionable if seeing the book as speeches or preaching is helpful
- Chroniclers style of this is so very different today
- Lack of criteria in defining the authors concept of a sermon
- The levitical sermons are never spoken by the Levites
- The frequency of the speeches is insufficient to apply the term to the book as a whole

### 4 Interpretation
Good about the view
- This combines the best of all the 3 views. It is interpreting the history, theology as a whole.
- almost like the gospels, that have 4 different account of Jesus and his ministry and focuses, Samuel and kings is 1 gospel and Chronicals the second gospel. different views, focuses and understanding.




Key themes
