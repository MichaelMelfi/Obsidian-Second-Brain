### Vanity (Ground floor)
Vanity literally means smoke
It means you cannot observe it but you can sense and know it’s there.
It’s like fleeting
Chase it and it will leave, you cannot grab it
It doesn't mean its meaningless, but that we don't get the meaning of it
Like trying to find the end of a rainbow.
Eternity in our hearts
Always trying to chase something new and not being satisfied
Not satisfied with wealth
Toil - work, usually a negative or hard work
Death

The obserdity of life
1. We don't always get what we want
2. When we do, it either doesn't satisfy us or doesn't last
3. We don't get what we deserve

Let go of control and take life as it comes.
We must accept life as smoke.

## Ecclesiastes
Everything is never enough
- Life is absurd so stop trying to control it
- Life is a gift from God to be enjoyed so be present
- Life is Gods so fear Him and keep His commandments

The end of the book doesn't resolve anything, it just ends. 
which is exactly what the author is talking about throughout the whole book, nothing ever feels finished or satisfied.

The only thing that fully satisfies is Jesus.
He satisfied the wrath of God on us.
Through Him is satisfied peace
Through Him is satisfied Joy
Through Him is satisfied Love
Through Him is satisfied life in fullness
