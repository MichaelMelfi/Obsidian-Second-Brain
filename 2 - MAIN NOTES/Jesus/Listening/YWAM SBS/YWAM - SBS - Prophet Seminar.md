# Lesson 1
## The Role of a Prophet
Gods messengers
The goal for a prophecy is to call someone to holiness
the whole reason for the prophets (Books) is to point to Jesus

The time of the prophets (Books) were in the times of Kings to the return of Israel

**The prophets are:
- Sensitive to evil
- Challenging Injustice
- Using explosive and visual language
- Showing compassion
- Struggling with emotions and people
- Prayerful

**Their function:
- Covenant police and lawyers
Priests were representing the people to God
Prophets were representing God to the the people
- Common people anointed for a task
Farmers, priests, young, old, gentle, harsh, Cryers, rich, or poor
- Gods mouthpiece and messengers

**Prophets have:**
Mouths - deliver a spoken message from God
Ears - Heard Gods word and sees Gods visions
Eyes - Saw things from Gods POV, not from a human viewpoint
Heads - They are not puppets, Gods partners with people
Hearts - They felt what God felt
Hands - Using visual aids to make messages more memorable

## Oracles
Is the smallest unit of thought
3 types of oracles

### Lawsuit oracles
4 piece suit oracle
Like a court room setting

Summons - The call to appear on trial before God - Who is on trial?
Charge - The accusation - Why are they on trial?
Evidence - The proof for the charge - Usually a list of sins
Verdict - The judgment sentence - 

Amos 1:3

### Woe Oracle
AOD - Announcement of Distress (Who it is too)
ROD - Reason for the distress
POD - Prediction of Doom

Amos 5:18-27

### Salvation Oracle
Future Referred to - on that day or at that time
Radical change - Complete turn of the present situation
Mention of blessing - promise of restoration, abundance, future, hope and blessing for peace, health prosperity etc…
