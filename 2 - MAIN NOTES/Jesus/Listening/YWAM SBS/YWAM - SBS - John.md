The word here is LOGOS (closest Greek word is ‘reason’)
In the beginning was the reason, and the reason was with God and the reason was God.

When the OR reads this, they will think of Genesis.

![[image 88.jpg]]

In the beginning there was only God
Now John is showing that there is a beginning in Christ.

Just like God created everything in the beginning, this was the thing that changed everything. God sent the word.
This is paralleled as
God sent the word (Jesus) in the physical to create a new beginning.

What John is saying is just like the beginning of the world was the greatest moment in history.
Jesus coming down is at the same level of greatness as the beginning of creation and the universe.

John uses the same sentences as Genesis to show the greatness.

Genesis says that creation was just God speaking
John is saying that creation was made through Jesus

Genesis was showing how everything was created
John is showing the reason and purpose everything was created

Genesis shows how the light was created
John shows the reason there was light in the beginning

Genesis shows God making life
John shows Jesus is the life

Jesus didn’t just come to die for sin, but he came to reimagine everything and give everything that already was…reason and purpose.
WE ARE NOT THE REASON, HE IS THE REASON

We have value because we are created in the image of God.
