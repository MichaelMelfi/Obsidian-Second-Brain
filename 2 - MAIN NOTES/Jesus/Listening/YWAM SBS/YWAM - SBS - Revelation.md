# Revelation: **Intro & Apocalyptic Framework**

## **Personal Teaching Context**

### **Teaching Revelation**
- **Health struggle**: Taught Revelation while sick, nearly fainted; finished strong with high school students attending. [transcript]
- **Favourite book**: Loves teaching Revelation because it's difficult but can be simplified for understanding. [transcript]

## **Revelation's Core Nature**

### **Jesus Revealed**
- **Not foreign**: Revelation of Jesus Christ, not hidden mysteries; different format from familiar gospel message. [Rev 1:1]
- **Literature mix**: Contains epistle, prophecy (mainly second coming), and **apocalyptic** elements. [transcript]

### **Gospel Retold**
- **Historic events**: Covers Jesus' life, death, resurrection, ascension in symbolic apocalyptic format. [transcript]
- **Visual impact**: Symbols like seven-headed beast devouring baby create hopeless scenarios resolved by Jesus' victory. [Rev 12]

## **Book's Unpopular Themes**

### **Righteous Judgement**
- **God's justice**: Wrath removes injustice; beautiful because it makes everything right. [transcript]
- **Seven "no mores"**: Book ends with seven things that exist no more (death, sorrow, etc.). [Rev 21-22]

### **Sexual Purity**
- **Strong emphasis**: More sexual immorality warnings than any other NT book. [Rev 17-18]
- **Cosmic struggle**: Reveals unseen battle against darkness, evil portrayed as very evil. [transcript]

## **Threefold Enemy Alliance**

### **Ungodly Trio**
- **Prostitute culture**: Seductive culture (harlot) drunk on saints' blood, loved by all. [Rev 17]
- **Beast government**: Anti-God government harlot rides upon. [Rev 17:3]
- **False religion**: All tied together serving each other; no loyalty in kingdom of darkness. [Rev 17:16]

### **Current Examples**
- **Iran prayer**: Uprising against Islam shows heavenly battle; Persia historically Christian. [transcript]

## **Book's Purpose & Audience**

### **Hope for Persecuted**
- **Church reality**: Written for persecuted church facing this evil triad daily. [transcript]
- **Voice of Martyrs**: Recommended resource to support suffering brothers/sisters. [transcript]

### **Not Fear-Based**
- **Believers rejoice**: Saints worship throughout; fear indicates wrong interpretation. [Rev 19:1-6]
- **Earth-dwellers terrified**: Unbelievers curse God during judgements. [Rev 16]

## **Interpretive Guidelines**

### **Original Context**
- **First audience**: Must make sense to seven churches; can't see helicopters in locusts. [Rev 9]
- **Symbol definitions**: John defines symbols (waters = peoples); don't redefine them. [Rev 17:15]

### **Gospel Priority**
- **No new gospel**: Can't teach mark of beast overrides salvation by faith. [transcript]
- **Sealed believers**: Christians already sealed/marked by God, not forced mark. [Rev 7:3]

## **Key Characters & Images**

### **Good Guys (White)**
- **Jesus/Lamb**: Lamb 28x; sacrificial lamb who's also shepherd. [Rev 5]
- **Church images**: Lampstands (light), bride, New Jerusalem (city). [Rev 1,19,21]
- **God sovereign**: Throne central; God always rules despite chaos. [Rev 4-5]

### **Bad Guys**
- **Satan/dragon**: Defeated at cross, angry, seeks to devour. [Rev 12]
- **Two beasts**: Sea beast (government), land beast/false prophet (religion). [Rev 13]
- **Harlot/Babylon**: Ungodly culture/city; provides luxury. [Rev 17-18]
- **Earth-dwellers**: Unbelievers plugged into this age only. [Rev 3:10]

## **Apocalyptic Literature Characteristics**

### **Historical Context**
- **Jewish form**: Popular 200BC-100AD; seeks divine intervention for human crisis. [transcript]
- **Modern equivalent**: Like *Lord of the Rings* - cosmic battle imagery. [transcript]

### **Key Features**
- **Cyclical structure**: Multiple views from throne perspective, not linear. [transcript]
- **End-time focus**: Judgement of evil, salvation of righteous; turning of ages. [transcript]
- **Persecution context**: Written to encourage suffering saints. [transcript]

### **Vision Format**
- **One vision**: John records massive vision, commanded 10x to *"write what you see"*. [Rev 1:11]
- **Angel guide**: Angels explain symbols throughout. [Rev 17:7]

---

## **🔑 Key Takeaways**

> [!abstract] **Revelation's Purpose**
> - **Hope book**: Encourages persecuted church; Jesus ultimately victorious. [transcript]
> - **Practical living**: Shows how to live amid evil triad (government, religion, culture). [Rev 17]

> [!abstract] **Interpretive Guardrails**
> - **Original audience**: Must make sense to seven churches first. [transcript]
> - **Defined symbols**: Use John's definitions, not speculation. [Rev 17:15]
> - **Gospel first**: Cannot contradict salvation by faith. [transcript]

> [!abstract] **Spiritual Reality**
> - **Two kingdoms**: No fence-sitters; kingdom of light vs darkness. [transcript]
> - **Sealed believers**: Christians marked/protected from wrath. [Rev 7:3]
> - **Sovereign throne**: God rules despite appearances. [Rev 4-5]

> [!abstract] **Cosmic Victory**
> - **Cross defeat**: Satan fell at Jesus' life/death/resurrection. [Rev 12]
> - **Present tension**: Age-to-come invaded present evil age. [transcript]

# Revelation: **Review & Cyclical Structure**

## **Apocalyptic Literature Review**

### **Symbol Interpretation**
- **John defines**: Symbols explained by John himself; no guesswork needed (40+ definitions). [transcript]
- **Whole-book reading**: Read Revelation in one sitting reveals symbol meanings (like "place" in Acts). [transcript]

### **Encouragement Focus**
- **Hope giver**: Encourages believers amid **present evil age** tension. [transcript]
- **Future promise**: Jesus' return brings new creation beyond imagination. [Rev 21-22]

## **New Creation Vision**

### **Perfected Skills**
- **Beyond current**: New earth allows skill development (golf, fishing, art, music) with perfect character. [transcript]
- **New senses**: Colors, sounds, music beyond human conception. [transcript]

### **Eternal Learning**
- **Skill growth**: Character perfected, skills still learnable; eternity for exploration. [transcript]

## **Book's Tone & Audience**

### **Not Scary**
- **Believers rejoice**: Scary interpretation = "earth-dwellers" (unbelievers) perspective. [transcript]
- **Earth-dwellers**: Fixated on present age only, no eternal viewpoint. [Rev 3:10]

## **Structural Approach**

### **Cyclical + Linear**
- **Repeated views**: Multiple perspectives from Jesus' first to second coming. [transcript]
- **Linear progression**: Moves toward second coming despite cycles. [transcript]

### **Chart Review**
- **Visual aid**: Students marking cyclical chart as instructed. [transcript]

---

## **🔑 Key Takeaways**

> [!abstract] **Reading Strategy**
> - **One sitting**: Read whole book reveals symbol definitions. [transcript]
> - **John's authority**: Use his symbol explanations only. [transcript]

> [!abstract] **Hope Perspective**
> - **Believers encouraged**: Not scared; earth-dwellers terrified. [transcript]
> - **New creation**: Skills perfected, senses expanded eternally. [Rev 21]

> [!abstract] **Structure Key**
> - **Cyclical views**: Multiple angles on same events (1st-2nd coming). [transcript]
> - **Linear goal**: Progresses toward second coming. [transcript]

# Revelation 8-11: **Trumpets & Witnesses**

## **Righteousness Accounting (Review)**

### **Hebrews Foundation**
- **Sins forgotten**: *"I will remember their sins no more"* (Heb 8:12, 10:17). [transcript]
- **David's blessing**: Righteousness written, sins *not written* (Rom 4:3-8). [transcript]

### **Great White Throne**
- **Believers clear**: Nothing but *"righteousness"* written for saints (Rev 20). [transcript]
- **Unbelievers judged**: Books record their deeds; Lamb's Book of Life decisive. [transcript]

## **Trumpets: Warnings to Repent**

### **Warning Purpose** 
- **Earth-dwellers targeted**: Warnings for unbelievers, not sealed believers (Rev 8:13). [transcript]
- **No repentance**: Refuse to abandon idolatry, sorcery, immorality, thefts (Rev 9:20-21). [transcript]

### **Sexual Immorality Defined**
- **Porneia scope**: Includes all OT sexual sins (Acts 15 Jerusalem Council). [transcript]
- **Sorcery/pharmakeia**: Mind-altering drugs open demonic doors (marijuana included). [transcript]

## **Trumpet Judgements (Rev 8-9)**

### **1st Trumpet: Hail/Fire**
- **Drought warning**: Like Elijah's 3-year drought (1 Kings 18); *"burnt up"* = no rain. [transcript]

### **2nd Trumpet: Sea Blood**
- **Volcano imagery**: Mountain thrown into sea; echoes Jeremiah's Babylon judgement (Jer 51:25). [transcript]
- **Empire falls**: God topples trusted human kingdoms. [transcript]

### **3rd Trumpet: Wormwood**
- **Bitter waters**: Life hardships drive people to God (Jer 9:15). [transcript]
- **Refugee gospel**: God moves people to gospel-accessible places. [transcript]

### **4th Trumpet: Darkness**
- **Spiritual warning**: Like crucifixion darkness; 1820 "no summer" volcano winter. [transcript]

### **5th Trumpet: Demonic Locusts**
- **Sealed protected**: Attack only unsealed; demonic manifestations drive to Jesus. [transcript]
- **Real stories**: Ouija boards, cemetery rituals, Taoist dedications broken by Jesus' name. [transcript]

## **Historical Examples**

### **Leipzig 1989**
- **Prayer revolution**: Monday prayers → 500,000 marchers → Berlin Wall falls. [transcript]
- **China response**: *"Church did it, won't happen here"* - God raises/fells nations. [transcript]

### **Afghanistan Church**
- **Prophetic warning**: Bulldozed church → government fell to Russians. [transcript]

## **Church Interlude (Rev 10-11)**

### **Little Scroll (Rev 10)**
- **Sweet/bitter**: Gospel sweet to proclaim, bitter when rejected (Ezekiel parallel). [transcript]
- **No delay**: Mystery fulfilled at second coming (Rev 10:6-7). [transcript]

### **Temple Measured (Rev 11:1-2)**
- **Protected church**: Temple/altar measured; outer court trampled 42 months. [transcript]
- **Suffering age**: Same timeframe as seals (3½ years = church age). [transcript]

### **Two Witnesses = Church**
- **Lampstands/olive trees**: Church testifies through church age (Rev 11:3-4). [transcript]
- **Moses/Elijah powers**: Fire from mouth, no rain, blood waters, plagues. [transcript]

### **Witnesses' Fate**
- **Testimony finished**: Beast kills after mission complete (Rev 11:7). [transcript]
- **World rejoices**: Earth-dwellers celebrate 3½ days, exchange gifts (Rev 11:9-10). [transcript]

> [!abstract] **Believer's Record**

> - **No condemnation**: Already declared righteous before Christ (2 Cor 5:10). [transcript]

> [!abstract] **Trumpet Purpose**

# Revelation 17–20: Harlot & Second Coming

## Revelation 17:1–6 - **Harlot Introduced**

### **Angel's Invitation**
- **Waters meaning**: Great prostitute on waters *(peoples, nations)*; kings immoral with her (Rev 17:1–2,15).
- **Scarlet beast**: Rides beast with seven heads, ten horns (Rev 17:3).

### **Luxurious Evil**
- **Rich appearance**: Purple, scarlet, gold cup of abominations; *"Mystery Babylon"* (Rev 17:4–5).
- **Saints' blood**: Drunk on blood of saints (Rev 17:6).

## **Harlot's Identity**

### **Deceptive Culture**
- **False beauty**: Seduces kings; vomit-worthy evil (Rev 17).
- **Secularism**: *"Be true to yourself"*; no absolutes; happiness goal (Rev 17–18).

### **Immoral Trade**
- **Human souls**: Sells slaves, sexual chaos, drugs (Rev 18:13).
- **Anti-God ally**: Rides ungodly government (Rev 17–18).

### **Church Warning**
- **Come out**: Separate from deception (Rev 18:4).

## **Revelation 17:7–14 - **Beast Explained***

### **Seven Heads**
- **Kingdoms**: Seven mountains/kings: five fallen, one is, one brief (Rev 17:9–10).
- **Eighth beast**: From seven, doomed (Rev 17:11).

### **Ten Horns**
- **Brief power**: Ten kings aid beast one hour; war on Lamb, defeated (Rev 17:12–14).

## **Revelation 17:16–18:24 - **Harlot Destroyed***

### **Betrayed**
- **Beast turns**: Horns, beast burn her; darkness disloyal (Rev 17:16).

### **Global Mourning**
- **Kings weep**: Luxury lost in one hour (Rev 18:9–19).
- **Heaven rejoices**: No sorcery, blood found (Rev 18:20–24).

## **Revelation 19:1–10 - Marriage Supper***

### **Hallelujah Chorus**
- **Multitude praises**: God judges prostitute justly (Rev 19:1–5).

### **Bride Ready**
- **Fine linen**: Righteous deeds granted as dress (Rev 19:7–8).

## **Revelation 19:11–21 - **Second Coming (Third View)***

### **Christ Returns**
- **White horse**: Faithful, True; *Word of God*, King of kings (Rev 19:11–16).
- **Sword mouth**: Strikes nations; blood-dipped robe (Rev 19:15).

### **Beast Defeated**
- **Lake fire**: Beast, false prophet thrown alive (Rev 19:19–21).

## **Revelation 20:1–6 - **Satan Bound***

### **Thousand Years**
- **Pit chained**: Cannot deceive nations *(symbolic, post-cross)* (Rev 20:1–3).
- **Gospel era**: Gentiles reached (Acts 26:17–18; John 12:31–32).

## **Revelation 20:7–10 - **Final Battle (Fourth View)***

### **Satan Loosed**
- **Gog/Magog**: Deceives nations, surrounds saints (Rev 20:7–9).
- **Fire ends**: Devil to lake of fire (Rev 20:10).

> [!abstract] **Harlot Dangers**
> - **Deceptive culture**: Secularism seduces like harlot (Rev 17–18)
> - **Come out**: Church must separate (Rev 18:4)

> [!abstract] **Battle Certainty**
> - **Four views**: Same second coming victory (Rev 17:14, 19:19–21, 20:9–10)
> - **Christ conquers**: Lamb overcomes all (Rev 17:14)

> [!abstract] **Millennium Now**
> - **Satan bound**: Cross limits deception (Rev 20:1–3)
> - **Living now**: Gospel era between comings (John 12:31–32)

> [!abstract] **Church Destiny**
> - **Bride prepared**: Righteous deeds granted (Rev 19:8)
> - **Foes doomed**: All enemies destroyed quickly (Rev 17:16, 19:20, 20:10)
