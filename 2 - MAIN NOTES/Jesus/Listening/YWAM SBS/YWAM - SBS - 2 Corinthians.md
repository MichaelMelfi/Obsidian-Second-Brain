### Dating
50AD - Paul visits Corinth and establishes the church Acts 18
52AD - Paul leaves Corinth Acts 18:18-22
52AD - Paul stays in Ephesus and writes the new lost letter A.
This letter Paul told them not to associate with sexual immoral people (1 Corinthians 5:9) This letter most likely carried by Timothy (1 Corinthians 4:17)
Paul being absent left vacuum that was filled with ‘Worldly’ speakers (1 Corinthians 4:18-20) Paul heard of this by a report given by ‘Chloe’s people’
The Corinthians wrote Paul a letter where they asked for his wisdom (1 Corinthians 7:1)
54-55 Paul wrote 1 Corinthians from Ephesus responding to the report and letter.

Paul changed his plans to travel which he laid out in 1 cor 16:5-7 which upset the Corinthians. Paul also faced life threading circumstances somewhere around this time 1 Corinthians 15:31-32, 2 Corinthians 1:8-10
Timothy returned from Corinth with bad news which made Paul make an emergency visit to Corinth (2 Corinthians 2:1, 13:2)
Paul was bitterly attacked during his visit, with a member of the church opposing him and the church standing back and not defending him (2 Corinthians 2:1, 5, 7:12)
Paul fled from Corinth and stayed away because he did not want another painful visit (2 Corinthians 1:23)

Paul wrote them a sorrowful letter (Lost letter C) 2 Corinthians 2:3-4, 7:8-9, 7:12 (Some say that this is apart of 2nd Corinthians)
Paul waited anxiously for the news of how this letter was received as Titus took it to Corinth (2 Corinthians 2:12-13)
Although late Titus returned with good news saying that the letter was received well and caused the Corinthians to repent (2 Corinthians 7, 2:5-10)
Interlopers

Paul from Macedonia (2 Corinthians 7:5) sent Titus with 2nd Corinthians to prepare the church for their visit (12:14, 13:1) and to make sure they didn’t forget the collection (8:6)
Paul returned to Corinth around 56-57 (Acts 20:2-3) and writes Romans while there (Rome 16:23 1 Corinthians 1:14)
