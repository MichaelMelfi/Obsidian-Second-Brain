### Why did Jesus come when He did?
#### Language
- The Roman Empire when Jesus came was prosperous everyone spoke the same language.
- Before them it was Greek (They forced everyone to learn Greek which had never happened before)
- Before that it was Babylon
- Lots of theological + philosophical language and concepts of being ‘saved’ etc already in the Greek language


#### Travel
- More travel than any other empire
- Very well built roads throughout whole empire
- Travel by foot, donkey, horse, carriage or ship
- Lodging places at regular intervals
- Trade more encouraged = Grater common ground

#### Pax Romana
- Roman Empire really valued and wanted peace
- There was peace, safety and unity throughout the region due to military presence
- Was easy for people to travel unlike before as it would have been dangerous and most likely be robbed
- This helped the missionaries

#### The Perfect Environment
- Language - Everyone spoke the same language
- Travel - Travel was easy and safe
- Pax Romana - People were already thinking of Theosophical things
