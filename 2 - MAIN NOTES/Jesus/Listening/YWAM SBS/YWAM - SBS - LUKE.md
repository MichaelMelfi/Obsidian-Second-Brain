- Allied with Rome - had privileged socieatal position and assisted Caesar
- Free exercise of Religion and kept cultural customs: Sabbath, circumcision, Law
- Exemption from military: protection in observing Sabbath: daily empower sacrifice to prove loyalty.
- Tax collection
1. Despised for collusion with Rome
2. Taxation symbol of being a conquered nation
3. Considered ritually unclean
This links to when the Caesar wanted to take a census on everyone’s names etc so he would know who to tax.

## Parables
To all on the outside everything is said in parables Mark 4:11
- Short illustrations/stories meant to illustrate a truth
- Often have an element of suprise
- Meant To provoke a reaction
- Reveal message to open mind, conceal message to hard-hearted
- The Jews were seen as high up veery smart people, parables expose their wisdom as the Jews would now understand stand it, however, a blind beggar or a woman understands it.

Types of parables
- True - simple story with a plot E.G. good samaritan
- Similitude - Short illustrations E.G. the parable of the sower
- Parabolic saying (Like a metaphor) E.G. salt of the earth

How to observe parables
1. Identify what type of parable it is
2. Identify ‘suprise’ in story
3. Identify points of reference
4. Identify which characters the OR would relate with in story
