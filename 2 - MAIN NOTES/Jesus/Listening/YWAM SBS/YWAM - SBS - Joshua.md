# The Land
All throughout the Torah it was promised and they were prepared to enter it.
Every book focused on preparing them differently.
Genesis - covenant
Exodus - making them free and a Holy people
Liviticus - making them Holy/law
Numbers - waiting for the generation to die
Deuteronomy - reminding them of all that God had done for them and what they must do going in there

### Who was Joshua?
Head of the army (Ex 17:8-16)
Assistant (Ex 24:13)
Name change Hoshea (Salvation) to Joshua son of nun(Num 13:16)
Successor as leader (Num 27:12-23)
Bold and faithful (Num 14:6)
Filled with spirit of wisdom (Deut 34:9)

Hoshea means ‘salvation’
Joshua means ‘Yahweh is salvation’ in Hebrew
‘Jesus’ is Aramaic is ‘Joshua’

This is a mirror of How Joshua led Israel into the promised land and conquers
