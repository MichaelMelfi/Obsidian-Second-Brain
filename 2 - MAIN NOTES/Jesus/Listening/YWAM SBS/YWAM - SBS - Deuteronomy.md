### Treaties
**==Deuteronomy is a vessel treaty from God to Israel==**
1. Preamble (1:1-8) - introduces the king and vassal making the covenant
2. Historical prologue (1:9-4:43) - The suzerain restates the benefits he has granted
3. Stipulations (4:44-26:19) - What is agreed upon, usually the largest section
4. Sanctions (blessings and curses) (27:1-29:1) - Obedience vs disobedience
5. Oath of allegiance (29:2-30:20) - How does the covenant continue in the case of death
6. Witness clause (31:1-32:47) - Different witnesses outside of the ones making covenant witness to it being made
