# YWAM - SBS - Philemon

Be a Paul to someone’s Onesimus.

Paul writing a load of letters in Rome for the different City Churches.
Philemon is a house church in the city of Colosai and is the only letter that is for a specific house church.
Omnisius and Tychicus bring the letters from Rome to Colosai.
Omnisius is a run away slave - it is illegal to keep him as a run away slave.
Omnisius is either walking to his death or life talking this letter back to Philemon who he ran away from.

This letter is a Letter of Recommendation:
(The only one in the bible)
Author
Recipient
Blessing
Opening appeal
Main argument
Support argument
Summary
Closing remarks

This letter is life or death to Omnisius, this is why every verse is significant.

## Application from the Characters
### Paul
- An example
- Challenges Philemon
- Always pointing back to Jesus
- Going against the norms
- Paul is imitating Jesus as he says for others to imitate himself.
- Paul lost his rights as he was in jail but not his influence. Use your influence for good.
- Seek unity in the body of Christ at all costs (He would prefer unity to his own possessions. He was going to lay down his own possessions for Onesimus.)
- Don’t take the easy route (He sent back Onesimus even though is was harder.)

### Philemon
- Philemon actually hasn’t done anything wrong, he is actually the victim.
