## Marriage customs
**Selection for the bride**
- Parents usually choose
- Young age of marriage 12-13
- Love who you marry. Marry first

**Betrothal agreement**
- A contract or covenant
- Seen as married after this
- Groom pays bride price
- Usually done by representatives
- Celebrated with feasts and gifts

**Waiting Period**
- Usually 1 year
- Groom prepares home for bride
- Bride prepares for marriage

**Wedding ceremony**
- Whole community is involved
- Bride washes = ritually purified
- Bride and groom wear crowns
- Bride wears white, jewels and veil
- Groom and friends bring back the bride with sining
- After feast, led to the marriage chamber while guests continue to party
- After consummation, festivities continue for a wee

**New family**
- Husband feeds and provides clothing for wife
- Both are faithful and raise children together
