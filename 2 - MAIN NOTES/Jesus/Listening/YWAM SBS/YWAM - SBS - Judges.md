## The book
The whole book is not chronological.
It was not meant to be historical account but to prove a point of what life or the world is like without God.
Chapter 2:11-21 explains the whole book

The cycles are:
Sin
Slavery
Supplication
Salvation
Silence

This is the same as us in sin.
We sin
We become a slave to it
We become aware of it and cry to God
Get saved from it
Then forget about God and sin again

It was only once they needed God they actually cried out to Him.
They should be wanting Him always and should be their first thought not an after thought.
