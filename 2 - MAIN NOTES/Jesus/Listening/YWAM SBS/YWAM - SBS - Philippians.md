### Roman Culture
Honour and shame culture
Everything was done for honour
Paul is writing to a honour and shame culture for Romans and Jews.
- Honour is directly to a persons social worth
- Honour = high public rating
- Shame = Low public rating
This is why Paul focuses on Jesus being in all authority but come down from heaven to humble himself and save mankind.
Guilt = I made a mistake
Shame = I am a mistake

How to get honour
Inherited - Family status, rank, class
Ascribed - Victories, speaking skills, achievements, benefactions

This is why brothers is mentioned so much, by calling someone brother, you are bestowing the Honour you have on them.
If you were a ‘brother’ of the emperor then that would be something they boast on.
WE ARE BROTHERS OF CHRIST, This would be mind blowing to them in this culture.

How to be shamed
Excommunication and banishment
Subside (This was honourable as it was taking the shame away from the family)
- Crucifixion = one of the most shameful things (They would not even crucify Romans)

Jesus came down and received shame and died for shamed people, this would have been absolutely crazy to them.
That God cam down and took on all shame but then was exalted above all.

Emperor worship and imperial Cult
- Believed emperor received divine status after death
- Caesar Augustus initiated the worship of Caesars
- He was seen as God
