### What is wisdom?
If you had the knowledge that a pot was very hot and you didn't want to burn myself.
would it be wise to pick up the pot bare hand?

Hebrew - Chokhmah - not knowledge but practically applying what you know
Ability to use the knowledge you have well

Farming
Building
Leading
Speaking
Reading the bible
Making decisions

**Biblical wisdom**
Gift Only from HS
The more you fear God the more you act upon what God wants
Deepest form of Wisdom is having a relationship with Jesus
Without a relationship with Jesus
You don‘t have the HS, 
you cannot fear the Lord, 
without fearing the Lord you will not act out what he says

This is why you have people who have so much knowledge about the bible even unbelievers but because they don't actually follow what God says, they are not wise, they are just full of knowledge that is wasted.
### Who has wisdom?
Who here has wisdom?
EVERYONE! EVERYONE WITH THE HS HAS WISDOM
Not about Age or smarts.
Still your choice to act it out.

Solomon 1 kings 3:10
God gave him wisdom and he was the wisest king.
Not because of how smart he was but because of the way he governed, judge and lead his people, he brought Israel into its 2nd Golden age.
He wrote the wisdom literature
Yet Solomon still sinned and went against God showing that even though you may possess wisdom, it is a daily act to act on the wisdom God gives you.

### Why is wisdom important?
Live better lives
Help others
We are called to HOLINESS
SET APART
Israelites were meant to be set apart, they had all the knowledge about God yet because they did not act out what they knew, they were not wise and therefore not set apart and not Holy!

As Christians we are to be Set apart and be Gods images/representatives on earth to the lost.
The way we can do this is to make the decisions in life that most glorify God and bring heaven to earth.

This happens through the help of the Holy spirit and discernment.
True biblical wisdom is being able to not just follow what God says from the bible but to discern what is most Holy in the times where it’s not clear.

### How can we get wisdom?

Reading the bible
- applying what it says! Medicine analogy

Experience
- Through life you are able to experience so many things that can help you choose what is right next time
- actually trying things out
- Don't be afraid of failing!!

Prayer
- James 1:5
- Only happen through the HS, this doesn't mean you will suddenly do everything right but that you will be able to have the wisdom to discern whats right.
- Discernment is the HS telling you something, the funny feeling when you do something wrong, thats discernment of the HS to warn you this is bad.

FEAR OF THE LORD
Is the foundation of wisdom because if you fear the Lord
- You will have reverence of God
- You will accept Him as Lord
- You will have the Holy Spirit
- You will be Holy and set apart
- You will make the decisions that most glorify God.

So what we will do right now is pray just as James says and as for wisdom and understanding.
