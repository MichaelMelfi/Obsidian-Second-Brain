## Inductive vs deductive

Deductive:
More talking than listening
You have an idea and try to find scriptures to support that idea with no context.

Inductive:
More listening than talking
You go to the text first and get the idea from what the text and context says.

#### “God’s will is always easy”
Starting conclusion: If something is difficult, it probably isn’t God’s will.
Verses used:
- “My yoke is easy.”
- “The Lord will make your paths straight.”

#### “Christians will/can never feel afraid”
Starting conclusion: Fear is always sinful.
Verses used.
- “Do not fear.”
- “Perfect love casts out fear.”
- “God has not given us a spirit of fear.”

## The 3 steps of bible Study
The Holy Spirit is the best bible teacher full stop.
Before you ever start reading the bible, PRAY PRAY PRAY.
Invite the Holy Spirit in.

**Observation** - Look, Look, Look till looking becomes seeing
(Murder skit, the more time you look at the painting the more things you see)
Where’s wally/Waldo


**Interpretation** - Why, Why, Why
What does the text mean to the original reader?
Like languages, if i come to a french person as an English speaking person then i will just see them as speaking Jibberish but for a french person they understand what is being said.
Ask as many why questions as possible.
Putting on the shoes of the OR and walk in their steps
Reading a letter changing who wrote it and it receiving it changes the whole context of the letter and what the meaning is.

**Application** - Walk it like you talk it
Application is the whole point of the method.
THIS IS THE GOAL
There is no point reading and doing bible studies if it doesnt change you.
If it doesnt change you then you are reading it for the wrong reasons.
Like you get medicine from a doctor when you are ill you go and pick it up but never use the medicine and just carry on how you were.
