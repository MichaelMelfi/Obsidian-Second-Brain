# Sunshine Coast - Sanctuary Church - Sean Stanton -  When fear speaks, faith has to speak - be strong and courageous


Has the voice of fear and anxiety become louder than the voice of God?
When fear speaks, faith has to speak.: be strong and courageous

Matthew 1:23 - He will be named God with us
He is with us, not away from us.

It’s not the fire that leads you but how many logs are on the fire
When there is a circumstance, God actually steps into that circumstance when you praise him in it.

Fears biggest thing is to pause you for enough time that the time for the thing you were scared of passes and the opportunity to grow in that thing has gone.

1. Fear makes us hide from God
Adam and Eve.
They hide themselves from God when fear and shame came

GeNessis 3:9-10 - I was afraid so i hid.
God has never lost you, you are hiding from God and He wants to locate yourself.

2. Fear makes us hide our reality
Ill make you ruler over much.
Parable of the stewards.

Matthew 25:25 - I was afraid so I hid your Gold
Fear buries potential


3. Fear makes you hide from challeneged

Judges 6:2 - The Israelites prepared shelters (Hebrew is hiding places) for themselves in mountain Clefts, saves and strongholds

Fear makes you want to step back and hide from the battle


4. Fear makes us follow God at a distance

Luke 22:54 - Peter followed at a distance and ended up denying Jesus. (Went out and weeped bitterly)

Warming himself at a charcoal fire Jesus came to him.

5. Fear makes us doubt and question God

Gideon doing what he is doing in a wine press makes no sense.
Judges 6:12-13 - If God is with us, where is he?

Courage is the greatest of all virtues, but if you do not use courage, you will not be able to act out the rest. (Samuel Jackson)

Romans 8:15 - the spirit you received does not make you slaves, so that you fear again.

Ephesians 4:23 - renew you mind
Joshua 1:7-8 - Before Joshua faced an enemy, God commanded him to meditate on the word of God and you will be prosperous.
