# Anthem Church - Joy


> “And the angel said to them, “Fear not, for behold, I bring you good news of great joy that will be for all the people.”
‭‭==Luke‬ ‭2‬:‭10‬ ‭ESV‬‬==

There is 
==good news==
==Great joy==
==For all people==

Be ready in every season, In and out (2nd Timothy Chapter 4)
Be joyful is every season, in and out

It’s who and what your focused on that depends on the outcome of a season

It’s not true joy and peace if you are only those things when its easy
Joy is a fruit of the spirit

## Joy has a source
news isn't something we have done or have to do (instructions) it is reporting on something that has already been done.

Joy doesn't come when life feels different

The angel didn’t tell the Shepard's instructions to do to get joy
The angel told them the joy has come through a person

Joys source is the person of Jesus not the season.

When you truly believe grace, joy is the response.
Great joy grows when grace goes from a teaching to a belief.

Joseph was thinking of divorcing her this speaks to his character
Most others would stone her.

We believe that if we get the answers, we will then get the peace and joy.
The opposit is true, you get the joy and peace from know that even though you don't know why, God has the power and Gods plan will turn it for good.

The joy and peace is through the promise, not the answer.
Stop trying to work out the answer, try searching for the promise again and fix your eyes on that.

The things we don't understand they are in our mystery box.

## Joy is to be shared.
We are called to carry that message of Great joy to everyone around us.

Joy is not meant to end with you but is meant to flow through you.
Your goal is not to get joy, it is to give and portray joy.

It doesn't say produce the fruits of the spirit.
It says walk in the fruits.
This means you already have it there, so walk in it and act like it.

It’s interesting that God chose to call it a fruit.
Fruit is meant to be picked and eaten, from the tree.
It is meant to be shared with people.
The more that is taken the more it can give.

We can constrain joy as an angel moment instead of a fruit.

Sometimes we only see joy as a perfect moment but God is seeing it in a stable moment. (Not a 5 star hotel but a stable for Jesus’ birth) (The most joyful moment on the world and it was done in the lowest place.)
