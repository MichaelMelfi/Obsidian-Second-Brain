##### Righteousness:
 ✝️ Christian Definition:

**Righteousness** is the **state of being justified before God**—not because of our own goodness, but because of what **Jesus Christ** did on our behalf.

---

🔑 Two Key Aspects of Christian Righteousness:

 1. **Imputed Righteousness** (our position before God)

- Through **faith in Jesus**, Christians are **declared righteous**—God treats us as if we had never sinned.
    
- We **don’t earn** this righteousness. It’s a **gift**.
    
- 📖 _“This righteousness is given through faith in Jesus Christ to all who believe.”_ — Romans 3:22
    

> This is sometimes called **justification**: being made right with God instantly through faith.

---

 2. **Practical Righteousness** (our daily life)

- After being saved, Christians seek to **live righteously**—loving others, avoiding sin, and obeying God.
    
- Not to _earn_ salvation, but to **live out** the righteousness we already have in Christ.
    
- 📖 _“Anyone who does what is right is righteous, just as he is righteous.”_ — 1 John 3:7
    

---

 🔄 Summary:

|Type|Meaning|How it comes|
|---|---|---|
|**Imputed Righteousness**|Being declared righteous by God|Through faith in Jesus|
|**Practical Righteousness**|Living rightly according to God’s ways|Through the Holy Spirit’s help|

---

 🙏 Bottom Line:

A Christian is **righteous** not because they’re perfect—but because they’ve put their **faith in the perfect One**, Jesus.  
From that place of grace, they then **grow in holiness** day by day.
