##### 🔤 **Breakdown of the Name:**

- **יְהוָה** (_YHWH_ or Jehovah) = God's personal name, "I AM"
    
- **יִרְאֶה** (_yir’eh_) = "will see" or "will provide"
    

> In Hebrew, **"to see"** often implies **"to see to it"** — meaning to take care of or provide.

So, **Jehovah Jireh** means:  
👉 **"The LORD will see to it"**  
👉 or simply, **"The LORD will provide"**
